Sarhisob · design system

Calm, evidential, dense where it has to be

One system for two audiences. The owner side uses more air, larger numbers and plain-language headings; the officer side tightens type to 13px, packs tables and keeps evidence one click away. Nothing is coloured unless the colour carries meaning.

Typography — IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono

Plex Sans for everything read; Plex Mono only for figures quoted from a source document (passages, extracted values), so a reader can tell "what the document says" from "what we computed". All numerals tabular.

Buttons & form controls

Text link →

One primary button per screen. Buttons are real <button> or <a>; icon-only buttons carry an aria-label. Language switch is a segmented control in the top bar; the interface language and the document language are independent.

Evidence references & AI conversation

A dashed chip marks any figure or claim that can be traced: or . Clicking opens the evidence pane with the document, page, the matched passage (highlighted) and an interpretation. Plain numbers without a chip are computed from chipped ones.

AI

Direct answer first, in one sentence with the key figure.

Then the supporting table or bridge, each row pointing at a record. Then "what I can't see" in an amber box when data is missing. Then numbered next steps, cheapest first. No greeting, no sign-off, no emoji.

What I can't see. States the missing document and what uploading it would change.

  • Uppercase 11px headers, right-aligned numerals, hairline row rules, totals on surface-2 with a stronger top rule.
  • Charts: two series at most — accent for "ours/now", warm grey for the comparison; one axis; recessive grid; a legend plus direct labels on the latest values only.
  • Status colours appear in charts only for thresholds and status counts, never as a third series.
  • Every chart answers a question stated in its heading ("Is revenue growing faster than expenses?").
